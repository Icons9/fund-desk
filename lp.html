Understanding your numbers

Why is DPI zero?
Nothing has been sold yet — distributions follow exits, typically from year 3–4. We manage for cash returned, not paper marks.
Why "n/m" for IRR?
IRR on months-old cash flows is noise. We report it once it becomes meaningful — and we will never lead with it over DPI.
Benchmark
Quarterly reports compare against healthcare PE peers of the same vintage once peer data exists (source: industry benchmarks).
